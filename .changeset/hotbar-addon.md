Add hotbars for abilities, consumables and game actions.

- Bind typed action IDs to slots with live availability, rejection reasons, quantities and cooldown reads from their existing owners.
- Assign, clear, swap and save slot layouts through the headless model, or use the controller's keyboard, gamepad, mouse and touch controls.
- Customize the default view or supply a presenter, and inspect assignments and activation outcomes through the existing Inspector and entity events.
