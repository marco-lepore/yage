Add typed loot tables with explicit seeded randomness, weighted pools, quantity ranges, nested tables, eligibility conditions and draws without replacement.

Roll results include reward origins and candidate explanations. Definitions are validated before use, including nested references, cycles and work limits. An optional LootComponent uses scene randomness and exposes completed rolls and callback failures through the existing Inspector. Games retain item definitions, inventory acceptance and saved chest progress.
