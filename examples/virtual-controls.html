Virtual Controls Addon

On-screen controls from @yagejs-addons/virtual-controls: a floating stick (left half) plus action buttons that drive the same actions as the keyboard (A/D or arrows, Space jump — hold it for a higher arc, Shift dash). The overlay defaults to visible: "auto" (mobile only); this page forces it on so you can drive it with the mouse. Tap the backdrop to ripple — touches on the controls are consumed and never ripple.
