Behavior Trees

Two guards share one tree. Each walks to the gate at the end of its lane, waits a random reaction time, attacks, and recovers. The gate flashes on every strike, orange for a heavy one. A weighted choice picks a normal or heavy attack. Heavy attacks also pass a chance check and have a four-second cooldown. Low health interrupts whatever the guard is doing. It runs back to the green circle while calling for help, rests, heals, and returns. Damage hits A during an attack and B during its approach at the start. Each panel shows the running branch, the heavy-attack cooldown, and the last interrupted action. 1/2 damage a guard · Space disable or restart A's tree
