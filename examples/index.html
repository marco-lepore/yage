Interaction Addon

Synth Addon

Procedural sound effects (@yagejs-addons/synth): every preset synthesized at boot and registered with the audio engine under an alias — no audio files. Click a pad to hear it and see the rendered waveform, with detuned variants and a seamless ambient loop.

addon core renderer audio

Abilities Addon

Phase-based abilities + a hit contract (@yagejs-addons/abilities): a close-quarters brawl with swappable fist and kick loadouts, a lunging punch, a homing fireball, a buffered dash-cancel, a charge kick with bullet time, tap-dash/hold-run movement, a hold-block/tap-parry guard split, an item-lane potion, a zoomed-in follow camera, and a hotbar with per-action cooldown wipes. Enemies telegraph a melee strike or a fireball depending on range, with hitstop, camera shake, impact particles, and hit/parry/death SFX scaling with hit weight. Press E to swap loadouts or R to reset.

addon core renderer physics particles input audio
