Sprite materials

Move the mouse to carry the lamp. Up and Down raise and lower it; a low lamp rakes across the surfaces and a high one lights them from the front. The scene uses surfaceLighting(), so every sprite on a world layer is lit. The floor tiles and the "No maps" orb set no material at all. A normal map gives a sprite depth, a specular map gives it highlights, and unlitMaterial() opts a sprite out of lighting. The crate is normal-mapped and blocks light, so it casts a shadow over the floor and the orbs. Emission stays visible in its shadow but lights nothing around it. The animated orb switches its color, normal and emission frames together. Text and Graphics are not lit: the labels and the lamp marker keep their own colors. Tilemaps and particles are not lit either.
