Adaptive Music

Click the page or press a key to start audio. Every sound but one is a synth instrument rendered when the page loads; the accent is a recorded file. The hi-hat is a custom instrument the game defines. Raise danger to bring in bass, kick, hi-hat, and the recorded accent; the wind follows the same control. A reward plays a bell on the next eighth note, moved into the current chord. A new area switches between the swung waltz and straight four-beat music on the next bar, and a new lead instrument also waits for the bar.

Pausing the game pauses the scene and the music and ambience channels, so the music resumes on the beat where it stopped. Measuring renders five seconds of the current song offline and reports its sample peak and RMS. Removing the music entity stops its song and wind.
