Adaptive Music

Choose a lead instrument. Sounds are rendered during the build and loaded as files. Raise intensity to add bass, percussion, and a recorded accent. Change between a swung waltz and straight four-beat music. The chord cue joins on the next eighth-note grid line. Wind follows the same intensity control.

Offline statistics measure a five-second mix of music, a changing layer, and wind. Peak and RMS do not establish musical quality. Removing the audio owner stops its sounds and controls; reload the page to create it again.
