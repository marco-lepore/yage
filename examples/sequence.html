Sequence Addon

One clip from @yagejs-addons/sequence animates two actors. Aster's positions scale with the stage rectangle. Ember keeps fixed offsets from the rectangle's bottom-right corner. At each event marker the actor makes a gesture, which the game receives as an entity event. When an actor says hello, the game pauses the clip for 0.75 seconds, the way a cutscene waits while a line of dialogue is on screen. The line under the stage shows the playback state.

Edit the clip in the editor's Sequence mode, then play the saved workspace or an exported runtime clip here. The game refuses a clip that names an actor slot it does not have, or a property or event its actors do not provide. The error appears below, and the current clip keeps playing.
