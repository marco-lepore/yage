Lighting

A warm lamp, a sweeping spotlight and an orbiting lamp share a room of solid furniture that blocks their light. The white probe moves through their falloff and reads LightingWorld.levelAt(). The HUD uses the same light data as the visual renderer. The address bar picks the look: ?renderer=soft for shadows with soft borders and a faded cone edge, ?renderer=hard for the overlay's single hard line. ?size= is the lamp diameter in pixels, ?softness= the share of the spotlight's spread its edge fades over, ?bounce= how strongly bounced light shows, ?blend=mix to blend toward it rather than keep the brighter of the two, ?res= the light buffer's density, and ?lights= adds that many drifting lamps. soft, wide lamps · hard, wide lamps · bounced light
