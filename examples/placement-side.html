Choose an object, move its preview, then click to place. Green means ready; red explains what to change. Furniture rests on the ground or on a floor section. Lamps sit on tables; pictures attach to wall panels. Esc cancel · G grid/free · Z/Y undo/redo
More controls: editing, finishes, permissions and saves
Click an existing object to move it. D duplicates it and its attachments. Delete removes one object. X removes it with its attachments. F/W change the room floor/wall finish. C changes selected furniture. P locks editing. S/L save/load this view in your browser. Add ?debug=1 for Inspector control.
