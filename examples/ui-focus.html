Keyboard & Gamepad Focus

Arrows / WASD / d-pad / left stick move · Enter or A confirms · Esc or B cancels · hold a direction to repeat: first after 0.35 s, then every 0.1 s · Two focus looks, side by side: this page asks the plugin for an outline, so the grid, the steppers, the widgets and the save slots draw one just inside the focused element; the Main Menu rows pass focusStyle: null and put a > marker beside the focused row from onFocusChange instead. Walk the menu with ↑ ↓ to move the marker, then → into the grid to see the outline · Pointer focus: the Pointer focus stepper under Pointer in the Inventory pane steps none / press / hover on ← →, and the status panel prints the setting. On press, the default: move the mouse over a menu row and it tints while the marker stays where the keyboard left it, then click that row and the marker follows. On hover: moving the mouse across the rows drags the focus along with it. On none: neither a hover nor a click moves the focus, and the click still runs the row's action · Pointer ownership: Enter on Quit to Desktop or on a save slot opens the dialog, which takes the keys and the pointer. With Dialog owns the pointer checked, the default, the menu behind answers nothing: hover Continue and it stays untinted, click it and nothing happens. Enter on that checkbox clears it — open the dialog from a save slot and click Quit to Desktop behind it, and the question changes, because the click ran · Press: hold Enter and the row paints pressed, let go and it acts once — let go after moving the focus off and nothing runs · Tabs: ← → run the strip, ↓ leaves it · Menu: ↑ ↓ step over the disabled Upload Save row · Grid: all four directions move by position, and off an edge focus carries on into the pane next door · Wrap: ↑ from the tab strip comes round to the last menu row, and → from New Save comes round to the strip · Save Slots: these rows ask for a filled selected row as well as the outline; hold ↓ past the fold and the list scrolls to follow — then scroll it by wheel or drag and check the follow leaves your offset alone · Dialog: Esc closes it and focus returns to the row that opened it · Steppers: ← → change Music and Difficulty and keep focus, ↑ ↓ still move · Widgets: the slider and the radios step on ← → and release focus at their ends — the slider's outline frames the track and the handle together and holds still as the value sweeps · Enter toggles any checkbox, and the Apply button shows its pressed art while Enter is held and its resting art once you let go · Display select: Enter opens the list, and the open list takes the keys — ↑ ↓ move the lit row while the menu behind it stays still, Enter picks that row and closes, Esc closes on the value it already had · Enter on the save-name field types into it — letters go to the text, not the menu, and fire no action; the status panel names whichever of the two is holding the keys · F fires a gameplay action, and the status panel counts every Enter gameplay still hears
