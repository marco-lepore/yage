Render Targets & Blend Modes

C switch composite · R cycle buffer resolution · Space pause the orbiting light The lights are drawn into an offscreen buffer, then composited over the world. cut-out erases soft holes in darkness — true colours, no tint. multiply adds coloured lights into an ambient floor — tinted and accumulating, but every surface takes the lamp's colour. cut-out + glow uses the cut-out buffer plus a second additive overlay, for true colours and tint at the cost of two passes.
